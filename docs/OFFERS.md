# Offer sheets

**Move 1: design the offer around the buying decision, not the skill set.** One sheet per segment Lead Scout tests. Fill each sheet from what businesses in that segment actually told you (VALIDATION_PLAN.md, Conversation B, questions 6–8), not from guesses. Mark every line with where it came from.

**On platforms (ADR-0012), each offer sheet becomes a gig's packages:** fixed scope and fixed price in tiers, with the smallest safe first step as the basic package.

**Status:** empty until the first business conversations. Until the lawyer confirms your work status, these are drafts. No offer is sent or published.

**Source tags:** `[I-n]` = interview number n · `[R]` = a reason a business gave for saying no · `[G]` = guess, to be replaced.
